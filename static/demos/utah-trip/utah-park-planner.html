America the Beautiful pass

Private-vehicle entrance fees for US residents. One annual pass ($80) covers the pass holder and passengers in one private vehicle. Visiting from outside the US? The annual pass is $250, and Zion and Bryce Canyon add a $100 per-person nonresident fee. Check the source pages before you go.
