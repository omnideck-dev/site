omnideck · Workbench
why omnideck

Your model provider shouldn’t own your workbench.

Proprietary AI platforms often bundle the model, workspace, automation, and your history into one closed service. omnideck keeps the workbench open and on your machine. You choose what agents can access, whether each job uses a local or hosted model, and when work leaves your computer.

what omnideck does

Research, create, and automate from one workbench.

Put local agents to work across the web and your connected services. Keep the results as artifacts or schedule the work to run again.

  1. 01 —

    Research and act on the web

    Have an agent compare sources, collect information, and move through browser-based work while you watch the live browser or take over when needed.

  2. 02 —

    Create work you can use

    Turn the research into a report, spreadsheet, document, image, or working app. Each result stays in the workbench as an artifact you can inspect, refine, and download.

recurring work

Run useful routines

Schedule research, monitoring, reports, and other repeatable jobs. Review every run and its results from the workbench.

connected work

Complete work across your services

Connect email, calendars, cloud storage, contacts, and APIs so omnideck can take action across the services you already use—not just tell you what to do next.

security & ownership

The workbench runs on your machine.

You choose the files, services, and models an agent can use. omnideck keeps those boundaries local, visible, and under your control.

local by default

Your machine is the workspace

omnideck and its agent runtime run locally, so your workbench is not tied to a proprietary AI platform.

contained execution

Agents stay inside the boundary

Agent work runs in an isolated container with access limited to the shared files and system commands you allow.

protected credentials

Raw keys stay out of reach

API keys and OAuth tokens live in an encrypted local vault managed outside the agent process.

your connections

You decide what can leave

Choose when a job uses a hosted model or connected service. omnideck itself adds no telemetry or tracking.

your workbench

Set it up around the work you do.

Create reusable agents for different kinds of work, then choose the model and tools each one should use.

agent profiles

A specialist for each kind of work

Create agents for research, coding, writing, operations, or the work unique to you. Give each one its own instructions and tools, and carry useful project context into the next conversation.

model choice

Cloud and local models, side by side

Use recent open-weight models through Ollama, OpenRouter, or another OpenAI-compatible provider. Run models locally when they fit your machine and use hosted models when they fit the job.
